\subsection{A few equations}

The Gaussian integral is
\[
  \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} e^{-x^2}\,dx = \sqrt{\pi}.
\]

For positive integers, the sum of the first $n$ integers is
\[
  \sum_{k=1}^{n} k = \frac{n(n+1)}{2}.
\]

Finally, Euler's formula relates the exponential and trigonometric functions:
\[
  e^{i\theta} = \cos(\theta) + i\sin(\theta).
\]
